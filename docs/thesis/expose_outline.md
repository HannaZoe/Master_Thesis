## Open items for next session, roughly in priority order

1. **Length**: Section 1's dynamics paragraph is the main lever for
   trimming toward Wagner's length. Discussed cutting or shortening the Mix
   & Küfmann (2012) sentence specifically — least load-bearing of the four
   evidence pieces stacked in that paragraph. Not yet applied to the draft.

2. **Thermokarst paragraph revision, drafted but not yet confirmed.**
   Hanna's own field read: most dolines are shallow (only the cave-type
   features are deep) and no ice was visible at the floor of most mapped
   dolines during the August 2026 campaign. This doesn't disprove the
   thermokarst mechanism (Gude & Barsch's own explanation is that relict
   ice persists specifically *because* it's insulated under debris cover,
   i.e. not visible from the surface) but does support narrowing the claim:
   thermokarst is most plausible for the deeper, cave-like subset of
   features, not the population as a whole. A rewrite incorporating this
   was drafted in chat but not yet pasted into the working doc — replaces
   the vaguer "not mutually exclusive" hedge with an actual testable,
   field-grounded prediction. Revisit and decide whether to adopt.
